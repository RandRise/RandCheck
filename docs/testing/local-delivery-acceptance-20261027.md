## Short check when returning to the project

1. Open Home and continue a saved check. Confirm its invoice, purchase order and
   selected comparison are the ones you expect.
2. Read the outcome and counts. Open a missing check: it should explain the gap;
   recording a decision must not turn that gap into a pass.
3. Download the report and CSV. Check their filenames, comparison number, values
   and decision cutoff. Use new fictional files for a new end-to-end trial.

These steps are a convenience checklist, not another approval gate for the
delegated implementation work. Never reset a retained workspace to perform them.
