request for dark styling and a switchable light view. Rand explicitly chose the

## Change and scope

`apps/web/app/appearance-tokens.css` owns shared theme colours. Dark uses the
accepted charcoal/purple palette; Light uses pale grey, white and purple.
Readable link/focus accents are separate from filled buttons. Control outlines
are stronger than card dividers; warning/error/success colours retain their
meaning. Shared styling consumes those tokens, and Home keeps only its local
step-illustration colours. The workspace font now follows Home's Segoe UI choice.

The existing Appearance dropdown and preference implementation are unchanged.
Switching themes does not remount the workflow, submit a form or write documents.
Stored preferences, unavailable-storage fallback, Help, sign-out, permissions,
Arabic text, English digits, saved results and historical routes remain intact.
This batch changes shared appearance, not page layouts or comparison rules.

## Short manual check

1. On Home, choose Appearance → Light, then Dark. Compare the two views.
2. Open Document library or Continue a saved review. Check that your chosen
   theme stays the same. Reload the page to check that the choice is remembered.
3. While editing a review value, switch themes and confirm your typing remains.
   Save or discard the draft normally when finished.
